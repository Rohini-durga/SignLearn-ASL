About SignLearn

SignLearn is a browser-based ASL fingerspelling trainer. It uses MediaPipe Hands to map 21 points across your hand from your webcam feed, entirely on your own device — no video or images are ever uploaded anywhere.

How detection works

A geometry-based classifier looks at which fingers are extended or curled, and the distances and angles between key points, to match your handshape against known letter patterns.

Honest limitations

  • Live detection reliably covers 12 static handshapes: A, B, C, D, F, I, L, O, U, V, W, Y.
  • J and Z involve a traced motion and aren't supported by this static-pose detector.
  • Several letters (M, N, S, T, E, R, and others) look very similar in hand geometry and aren't reliably distinguished without a trained machine-learning model — they're included for reference and manual practice instead of live scoring.
  • ASL is a full language with its own grammar, not just English spelled letter-by-letter — this app teaches fingerspelling only.

Privacy

Your camera feed is processed locally in your browser and is never recorded, stored, or sent anywhere.
